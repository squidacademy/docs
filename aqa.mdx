## Learner Achievement Accreditation

### Overview

The AQA Unit Award Scheme (UAS) is a nationally recognized certification framework operated by AQA, one of the United Kingdom's largest and most established awarding organizations.

The scheme enables education providers to recognize and certify learner achievement through the successful completion of individual units of learning.

Unlike traditional qualifications that often require learners to complete an entire program before receiving recognition, AQA Unit Award Scheme certificates can be awarded throughout the learning journey, allowing students to build a portfolio of achievements over time.

# About AQA

AQA (Assessment and Qualifications Alliance) is an independent education charity and one of the UK's leading awarding organizations.

AQA qualifications and certifications are used by schools, colleges, training providers, and educational institutions throughout the United Kingdom and internationally.

The organization is recognized for its commitment to educational standards, assessment quality, and learner achievement.

# What AQA Unit Award Scheme Accredits

The Unit Award Scheme focuses on recognizing the successful completion of specific learning outcomes.

Each approved unit contains:

- Defined Learning Objectives
- Clear Success Criteria
- Measurable Outcomes
- Evidence Requirements
- Achievement Standards

Learners receive formal recognition once they have demonstrated that the required outcomes have been achieved.

# How the Scheme Works

As an approved Unit Award Scheme center, Squid Academy can create and deliver bespoke learning units aligned to its educational pathways.

Students complete learning activities and assessments that demonstrate achievement against clearly defined criteria.

When the required outcomes are achieved:

- Achievement is verified by the centre.
- Records are submitted through the Unit Award Scheme process.
- Learners receive an official AQA Unit Award Certificate.

This approach allows students to gain recognition for skills and knowledge as they progress rather than waiting until the end of a program.

# Benefits for Students

The Unit Award Scheme provides learners with tangible evidence of achievement throughout their educational journey.

Students benefit from:

- Official AQA Certificates
- Recognition of Individual Skills
- Increased Motivation and Engagement
- Clear Achievement Milestones
- Portfolio Development
- Evidence for Future Applications
- Continuous Progress Recognition

Each certificate represents a specific skill, competency, or area of knowledge successfully completed by the learner.

# Benefits for Schools and Partners

For schools, colleges, and training providers, the Unit Award Scheme offers a flexible method of recognising achievement without requiring learners to undertake traditional examinations.

Benefits include:

- Recognition of Practical Learning
- Flexible Assessment Methods
- Achievement-Based Certification
- Increased Learner Engagement
- Additional Student Outcomes
- Enhanced Programme Value
- Stronger Student Portfolios

The scheme is particularly effective for project-based, practical, and skills-focused learning environments.
