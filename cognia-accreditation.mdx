## **Institutional Quality Assurance**

### **Overview**

Cognia is one of the world's largest and most widely recognized educational accreditation organizations, serving schools, colleges, training providers, and educational institutions across more than 85 countries.

Unlike program-specific certifications that evaluate individual courses or learner achievements, Cognia accreditation evaluates the institution as a whole. It provides independent assurance that an organization operates according to internationally recognized standards of educational quality, leadership, governance, continuous improvement, and learner support.

For Squid Academy, Cognia represents the institutional layer of the quality framework, demonstrating that the organization itself meets rigorous standards beyond the curriculum it delivers.

# **About Cognia**

Cognia is a global non-profit organization formed through the merger of several long-established accreditation bodies in the United States.

Today, Cognia accredits and supports the following:

- K-12 Schools
- International Schools
- Private Education Providers
- Online Learning Institutions
- Training Organisations
- Educational Networks

Its accreditation framework is recognized by educational institutions, universities, ministries of education, and international school groups around the world.

# **What Cognia Accredits**

Cognia evaluates the overall effectiveness and quality of an educational organization.

Areas reviewed include:

- Leadership and Governance
- Strategic Planning
- Educational Quality
- Continuous Improvement Processes
- Learner Support Systems
- Staff Development
- Safeguarding and Student Welfare
- Operational Effectiveness
- Stakeholder Engagement
- Quality Assurance Procedures

The accreditation examines how an institution operates, manages quality, supports learners, and maintains standards over time.

# **The Cognia Accreditation Process**

Institutions seeking accreditation undertake a comprehensive review process that includes:

- Self-Evaluation and Reflection
- Evidence Collection
- Policy and Procedure Review
- Quality Assurance Assessment
- External Evaluation
- Continuous Improvement Planning

The process focuses on demonstrating that quality systems are embedded throughout the organization rather than existing only within individual programs.

# **Benefits for Students**

Students benefit from attending or participating in programs delivered by an institution that has undergone an independent quality review.

Benefits include:

- Greater Confidence in Educational Standards
- Consistent Learning Experiences
- Stronger Student Support Systems
- Improved Quality Assurance
- Continuous Programme Improvement
- International Credibility

Institutional accreditation helps ensure that learners receive a high-quality educational experience supported by effective organizational systems.

# **Benefits for Schools and Partners**

For schools, colleges, and partner institutions, Cognia accreditation provides assurance that the organization delivering programs operates according to recognized international standards.

Benefits include:

- Independent Institutional Validation
- Enhanced Reputation and Credibility
- Increased Stakeholder Confidence
- Stronger Quality Assurance Frameworks
- Evidence of Continuous Improvement
- Support for International Partnerships
- Alignment with Global Educational Standards

The accreditation demonstrates that quality is embedded throughout the organization rather than limited to individual programs.

# **Cognia and the Squid Academy Quality Framework**

Within the Squid Academy Accreditation Framework, Cognia represents the third and highest layer of quality assurance.

### **Layer 3 – The Institution**

**Key Question:** Is the organization credible, accountable, and quality assured?

**Accreditation:** Cognia

**Focus:** Institutional effectiveness, governance, quality systems, and continuous improvement.

This layer provides confidence that the organization delivering educational programs has the structures, processes, and oversight required to maintain high standards over time.

# **Why Institutional Accreditation Matters**

Curriculum quality and learner certification are important, but they do not assess how an organization is managed.

Institutional accreditation provides assurance that:

- Policies are implemented consistently.
- Quality assurance processes are monitored.
- Learner welfare is protected.
- Staff receive appropriate support and development.
- Continuous improvement is embedded in operations.
- Educational standards are maintained across all programs.

This creates a stronger foundation for long-term educational quality and sustainability.

The accreditation supports confidence in the institution's ability to deliver education that meets recognized international standards.

Cognia accreditation provides independent validation of an educational institution's quality, governance, leadership, and commitment to continuous improvement.
